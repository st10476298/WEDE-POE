Our Origin & History

Founded in 2023 in Pietermaritzburg, KZN Collectibles & TCG was established by local tabletop enthusiasts and collectors who recognised the need for a dedicated, tournament-grade hobby space in the KwaZulu-Natal Midlands. What began as a local card-trading network expanded into two full retail outlets serving Pietermaritzburg Central and Hilton.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To supply 100% factory-sealed, authentic trading card products, transparent secondary-market card valuations, and an inclusive environment for both casual collectors and competitive players.

Our Vision

To establish KwaZulu-Natal as a premier regional hub for sanctioned national card gaming circuits and tabletop strategy tournaments.

All sealed booster boxes, Elite Trainer Boxes (ETBs), and blister packs are sourced directly from official South African and European authorised distributors. Individual booster packs are drawn strictly from fresh factory-sealed display boxes in front of the customer and are never weighed or filtered.

We evaluate raw and graded single cards using live international benchmark data from PriceCharting, Cardmarket, and eBay Recent Sold listings, adjusted fairly for local ZAR landed shipping costs and Near Mint (NM) surface/centering inspection.
